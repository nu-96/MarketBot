# Blog Post Template

## Structure
1. **Title**: SEO-optimized, compelling, under 60 characters
2. **Meta Description**: 150-160 characters summarizing the post
3. **Introduction** (100-150 words): Hook + context + what the reader will learn
4. **Body Sections** (H2/H3 headers):
   - Each section: 150-300 words
   - Include examples, data, or quotes
   - Use bullet points and numbered lists
5. **Conclusion** (100-150 words): Summarize key points + CTA
6. **CTA**: Clear next step for the reader

## Guidelines
- Total word count: 800-2000 words
- Include primary keyword in title, first paragraph, and 2-3 subheadings
- Use secondary keywords naturally throughout
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
- Include at least one list or table
- Add image suggestions in [brackets]
