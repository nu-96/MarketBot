# LinkedIn Post Template

## Structure
1. **Hook** (1-2 lines): Start with a bold statement, question, or surprising stat
2. **Context** (2-3 lines): Set up the problem or opportunity
3. **Body** (5-8 lines): Share insights, tips, or story
4. **Key Takeaway** (1-2 lines): The main lesson or insight
5. **CTA** (1 line): Ask a question or invite engagement
6. **Hashtags** (3-5): Relevant industry hashtags

## Guidelines
- Word count: 100-200 words
- Use line breaks for readability
- Start lines with action verbs when possible
- Include emojis sparingly (1-3 per post)
- End with a question to drive comments
